[b]A modder's tool: ship a complete, ready-made ideoligion inside your mod.[/b] Design a belief system in RimWorld's ideology editor, save it, drop the file into your mod, and players pick it straight from the "Choose your ideoligion" screen, with every meme, precept, role, apparel rule, style, relic and narrative exactly as you built it.

RimWorld only ever offers ideologies it generates from memes, or ones loaded by hand from the local save folder, so a fully-authored ideoligion cannot normally travel with a mod. This framework is the missing piece that lets it.

[hr][/hr]
[b]For mod authors[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Four steps, no code:[/b] build your ideoligion in the in-game editor, save the .rid, drop it into an "Ideos" folder at your mod root, and depend on this framework.
[*][b]The card builds itself:[/b] your ideoligion appears as a card in a "Modded" group at the top of the ideology preset list, with its name and memes read straight from the file. No preset def to author, no naming convention to keep in sync.
[*][b]Loads the real thing:[/b] selecting a card loads your complete ideoligion, not an approximation regenerated from memes.
[*][b]Clean:[/b] nothing is written to the player's save folder. The chosen ideoligion is baked into the colony save like any other belief system.
[/list]

[b]Full setup guide and a worked example live in the README:[/b]
[url=https://github.com/wishrobber319/ideoligion-framework#readme]github.com/wishrobber319/ideoligion-framework[/url]

[hr][/hr]
[b]For players[/b]
Nothing to set up. If a mod you use depends on this, it is simply the library that makes that mod's ideoligion appear in your ideology list. No content of its own, safe to add or remove at any time. Requires the Ideology DLC.

[i]Custom ideology and ideoligion support for mod authors: shareable and preset ideologies, memes and precepts, belief systems, all through one modding framework.[/i]
